# Semantic Assurance Design — Specification V1

## Intent
Turn recurring assurance-harness friction into deeper reusable verification primitives without weakening governance.

## Source evidence
EREI-App-Google:
- EFF-20261015T180800Z-A29B7B59
- EPOCH-023 high rework/catch cohort
- repeated source-only L3 tasks involving stale bindings, allowlists, regex/source assertions, native process semantics and exact-head/provenance duplication.

## Scope
In scope: invariant extraction, semantic assertion design, shared assurance seams, measurement of rework reduction.
Out of scope: suppressing legitimate failures or replacing project-specific checks that have no reusable mechanism.

## Cross-project evidence

The mechanism is now independently corroborated in two materially different brownfield projects:

- EREI-App-Google — stale predecessor/successor bindings, allowlists and related assurance-harness friction during high-risk source-only work.
- EREI 3.2 — a bootstrap-only no-ledger rule had to become successor-aware before the first legitimate post-integration Task Learning event could be accepted.

This corroborates the **applicability/lifecycle** aspect of semantic assurance design. It does not yet prove effectiveness of every proposed shared primitive.

## Promotion requirement

Before Stable review, demonstrate on at least one bounded real assurance hotspot that the Candidate measurably reduces repeated harness friction without increasing verification-escape rate, final failed-task rate, or reducing invariant coverage.
